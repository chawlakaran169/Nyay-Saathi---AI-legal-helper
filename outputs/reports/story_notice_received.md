### What a legal notice is

A legal notice is a formal warning letter, usually from a lawyer, saying what the sender wants from you and by when. It is not a court order, and receiving one does not mean you have lost anything. But ignoring it can hurt you: the sender may go to court and point to your silence. Note the date you received it, read exactly what is claimed, and send a written reply within the stated deadline. Do not admit liability or promise payment before you check the facts and your documents. If the paper mentions a court date, 'summons', a police complaint, a bounced cheque or a bank loan, treat it as urgent and see a lawyer at once.
