### Evidence checklist: what to have ready

- ✅ Rental agreement (or any written rent terms)
- ✅ Proof you paid the deposit: bank/UPI record or receipt
- ⬜ Photos/videos of the home when you left
- ⬜ Your notice to vacate and the landlord's replies or excuses (chats/emails)
- ⬜ Proof you returned the keys / handed over possession
- ⬜ Final electricity/water/maintenance bills showing no dues
- ⬜ Your written demand, with proof it was sent (screenshot, speed-post receipt)

✅ = you told me you have it. ⬜ = not mentioned: look for it now. Keep originals and make copies; for chats and emails keep the original phone and take backups.
